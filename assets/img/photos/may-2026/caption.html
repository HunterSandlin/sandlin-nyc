May 5, 2026
It’s been a busy month for advocacy in New York!
The NYC Partners in Health Engage team held two fund raisers for PIH’s fight against tuberculosis. In the first we had 11 people volunteer at the Brooklyn Half Marathon. For every person volunteering from the same non profit, the organizers of the half marathon donate to them. We also had a lovely piano recital from our very own Beata Moon. The event was free with donations encourage, all of which going to Partners In Health. Both were a lot of fun! It was touching seeing all the runners at the half marathon complete a life goal of theirs. At the recital, Beata did an amazing job and performed some timely pieces including a a recently discovered one by Maria Anna Mozart!
Partners In Health Engagers also worked together with other local NYC advocacy groups for a New York State Hill Day in Albany! For hundreds of years it was illegal to buy sovereign debt, debt of other countries, with the intent of suing as soon as they default. In the late 90s, after having a law suit against Peru thrown out due to this law by a judge, a notorious hedge fund lobbied state legislator and added in a loophole that only prevents this practice for debt under $500,000. Which is to say, effectively all sovereign debt. We went to Albany to talk with our representatives about closing this loophole by passing the Champtery Bill.
NYC had a massive turn out to celebrate May Day! While often not taught in U.S. school and not recognized as a holiday here, May Day (international workers day) started in the U.S. and is celebrated in over 160 countries (that’s about the same as Christmas!). There were rallies, marches, pickets, protects, and celebrations across all five boroughs. The DSA help a rally opposing Amazon’s involvement with ICE, the IDF, and it’s treatment of workers. They then marched to Amazon's tech hub in midtown Manhattan and picketed outside along side unionized Amazon workers. There were multiple large events all over the city. Our Mayor, Zohran Mamdani, spoke at Washington Square Park which was the first time in nearly a century an NYC mayor recognized May Day. It was incredible seeing so many people show up in solidarity.
