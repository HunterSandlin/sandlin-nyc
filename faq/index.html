FAQ

Some common questions you might have about the site!

Why is the website so ugly?

Ouch, but fair. This site is designed after web 1.0 trends. Admittedly, there is still some JavaScript but it is still a static site. As pretty as some modern sites are, I got tired of the overly polished 3D websites with parallax and 10 JS frameworks. I also am tired of the overbearing algorithms and constant stream of content most social media platforms have build their websites around. This site is very much a reaction to the platform drive and frameworked bloated web 2.0 internet we now live in.


How do I leave comments?

You do not :(
Instead, if you like something you see, give me a call or email! My email is hunter@sandlin.nyc and if you already have my number, I would love to hear from you. Cold calls are always welcome from friends.


Can I make my own site like this??

You not only can but should! I'm working on making an easy guide to clone my website and host it for free. You will have to pay for a domain name if you want a URL that's readable, they're $5-$50 a year but you can also make a cool email if you buy a domain it so it's worthwhile! You will also have do a little very basic code but don't be intimidated, it's fun and easy!


I don't see links to any of your socails, where can I find them?

I have a LinkedIn I only use for work and have an Instagram I plan on deleting in the near future. Don't think the lack of links means you need to find them, this website is better than social links! My photos page is my Instagram and my articles page is my Substack! If you want to repost it, just send the link to a friend instead. If you want to leave a comment, just shoot my a text saying what you think.
